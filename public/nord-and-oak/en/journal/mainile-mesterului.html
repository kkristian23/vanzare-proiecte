A day in Ana’s workshop

Ana Muntean · 2026-05-13

From the first board to the passport signature.

Wood keeps responding to light, temperature and humidity long after it becomes furniture. This is not a flaw, but the nature of the material and the reason care should be matched to each surface.

Begin by observing

Use a soft, dry cloth for everyday dust. For marks, add a little water, work with the grain and dry the surface at once. Test any product in a discreet area and avoid universal sprays containing silicone.

A seasonal routine

As the seasons change, check joints, distance from heat sources and the condition of the oil finish. Small, timely interventions extend the piece’s life without erasing the beautiful patina of use.

Workshop tip: keep the passport care instructions beside your maintenance products.
