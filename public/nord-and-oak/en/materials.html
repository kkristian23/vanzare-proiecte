The material story

Every grain has an origin worth knowing.

Oak

Dense and tactile, suited to surfaces used every day. We select for grain and stability rather than perfect uniformity.

Ash

Elastic and expressive, ideal for chairs and curved components that can be repaired.

Birch

Light and efficient for children’s furniture and internal components.

Finishes

Water-based oils that allow maintenance and local renewal.
